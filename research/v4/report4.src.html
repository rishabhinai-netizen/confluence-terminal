The answer

Run three books with a third of capital each. Trend buys closes above the 55-day high, strongest relative strength first, and keeps a stop at the 50-day average. Swing buys closes above the 10-day high and sells at the next close after a close below the 10-day EMA. Results Leaders buys the top 10% of each results season and holds 60 sessions. 6. Execution, robustness and the gauge

When you sell matters

Without the market gauge

The gauge (equal-weight index above its 100-day average; more than half of stocks above their 50-day average; more 52-week highs than lows) scales new buys and never forces a sale. It costs little and halves the worst fall.

7. The live engine matches the research

8. Limits

  • Survivorship before 2025 (see above). The ranking of approaches is more reliable than their level; 2025–26 numbers are clean.
  • Costs are 0.3% each way. Stop fills before 2025 assume 0.5% slippage because opening prices are missing; from 2025 they use the actual open when a stock gaps through.
  • Winners are 35–45% of trades. Long flat or losing stretches are normal for every book.
  • Smart-money deals, filings and the concall tone fields cover about a year; they are watch flags until more history exists. StockScans valuation and cash-flow panels are still downloading under its rate limit.

9. The rules

  1. Universe: close ≥ ₹20, listed ≥ 200 sessions, market cap ≥ ₹1,000 cr (point in time), 20-day median turnover ≥ ₹5 cr where volume is known.
  2. Capital: a third each to Trend, Swing and Results Leaders. Trend and Swing have 10 slots each, Results 15. New buys are sized by the gauge: none at 0/3, ⅓, ⅔ or full.
  3. Trend: buy at the next close after a close above the previous 55 sessions' highest close; strongest 6-month relative strength first. Keep a stop at the previous session's 50-day average from the third session.
  4. Swing: buy at the next close after a close above the previous 10 sessions' highest close; strongest relative strength first. Sell at the next close after a close below the 10-day EMA, or after 30 sessions.
  5. Results Leaders: score each result against last season (growth ×2, trend, reaction); buy the top 10% within 10 sessions, skipping guidance cuts and stressed balance sheets; sell after 60 sessions. Details: Results Leaders research.
