Results Leaders

What the EquiSense, StockScans and price data can and cannot support; which famous approaches survive a fair test on Indian results since October 2022; and the one rule set the terminal now runs.

The answer in one paragraph

Buy the companies whose quarterly result lands in the top 10% of the previous season, judged on growth first and price confirmation second, hold each for one quarter (60 sessions), and add fewer new positions when the broad market is weak. 1. What the data can carry

A strategy test needs the signal recorded as it was known at the time, across several market phases. Most of the platforms' data is rich but recent, so only part of it can be tested as a strategy.

Results are counted when filed within 120 days of quarter end (later filings are restatements). Every rank is taken against the previous season only, so no figure uses information from after the trade date.

2. The legends, tested on identical plumbing

Every pool then runs through the same machine: buy within 10 sessions of the result at the close, 15 slots, sell 60 sessions later, 0.3% cost each way, the same market gauge, January 2023 to 12 October 2026. 9. Limits to keep in mind

  • Four years of usable history (October 2022 onward), mostly a rising small-cap market. 2025 was a losing year for every approach that buys stocks, the model included.
  • Prices before 2025 come from a set of stocks that are still liquid today, which flatters older results slightly (survivorship). The comparison between pools shares this bias, so the ranking of approaches is more reliable than the level of returns.
  • Costs are 0.3% each way. Before 2025 volume data is missing, so liquidity there rests on the ₹1,000 cr market-cap floor alone. Large orders in small companies would cost more.
  • Results season bunches the buys: the 15 slots often fill within two or three weeks and exit together a quarter later.
  • A 120-session hold matched the 60-session return with a smaller worst fall in this sample. The live rule stays at 60 until a longer record separates them.

10. The rule set

  1. Universe: results filed within 120 days of quarter end; market cap at least ₹1,000 cr; median daily turnover at least ₹5 cr where volume is known.
  2. Score each result against last season's results. Growth (revenue YoY, profit YoY, EPS change against price, margin change) counts twice; trend (distance from 200-day average, from 52-week high, 200-day slope, 6-month run against the market) once; the market's reaction to the result once.
  3. Buy only the top 10%, at the close, within 10 sessions of the first close after the result. Skip a guidance cut on the concall or a stressed balance sheet.
  4. 15 equal slots. New buys are sized by the market gauge: none at 0/3, a third at 1/3, two thirds at 2/3, full at 3/3. The gauge never forces a sale.
  5. Sell at the close 60 sessions after the buy. No stop-loss, no early exit.
