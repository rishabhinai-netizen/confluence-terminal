1 How this tool thinks

Three independent sources must agree before you act. AI-1 and AI-2 both score every earnings result — but they use different logic (AI-1/AI-2 correlation is ~0.64, AI-2 runs ~12 points higher on average). That gap is treated as a feature: when they agree, conviction rises; when they disagree, the stock lands in the Contradiction Log — never blended silently.

Beyond the two AI scores, two more confirming layers: Market momentum (25-day daily trajectory averaged across every index and industry the stock sits in), and the Signal Stack — how many of 26 pre-built screens (Sector Leaders, Promoter Buying, Institutional Bias Shift, Margin Expansion, etc.) the stock currently appears on. The Signal Stack is deliberately weighted toward ownership signals (promoter buys, institutional inflow) because those are the most independent from any AI reading of the earnings result.

Discipline that matters most: never act on one source alone. The Top Picks tab ranks stocks that already pass triple confirmation — but even those need you to open the intelligence card, read the actual verdict reason and the AI's own negative flags, and cross-check against the primary PDF filing before sizing.

2 The conviction formula, in the open

No black box. Each pick's score decomposes into 8 published parts, all visible when you expand its card:

  • Fundamentals (25%) — PAT YoY, OPM Δ bps, Rev YoY, Balance-Sheet Health
  • Sentiment (20%) — average of AI-2 Score and AI-1 Quality (they correlate 0.64, so pooled to avoid double-counting)
  • Guidance (15%) — Guidance Move (RAISED/CUT/etc), PEAD Index, presence of forward metrics
  • Momentum level (15%) + trend (5%) — current score + 5-day change
  • Signal Stack (10%) — weighted count across 26 AI-1 screens
  • Price confirmation (5%) — post-result price move
  • Confluence stamp (5%) — do all three sources agree directionally?

Then two multipliers: agreement × 0.85 to 1.10 (strong disagreement penalises, unanimous agreement bonuses), and freshness × 0.85 to 1.00 (results older than 15 days decay linearly to 45 days).

Platform Playbook
The workflow this tool was built to enforce.

1 Start with the Top Picks tab

Focus only on stocks that pass the triple-confirmation bar. There are typically 20-30 of these each quarter. This is your workable universe — not the 4,291-row raw list.

2 Expand every card before shortlisting

A high AI-2 Score is not a buy signal. Read the Verdict Reason (one line explaining why BEAT was assigned). Then read the NEGATIVE insights — the AI's own list of concerns. If the negatives are structural (retail losses continuing, cash burn, accounting cleanup), the number lies.

3 Cross-check the Signal Stack

A stock on Insider Trading Buying + Institutional Influx + Alpha Leaders + Margin Mavericks is materially different from one with high AI-2 Score alone. Ownership signals (gold badges) are independent from AI sentiment and carry the most weight in the Signal Stack composite.

4 Verify against the primary source

Every card links to the actual BSE PDF filing and the management audio recording. Two minutes reading the source beats trusting any AI. Especially non-negotiable when the pick sits above ₹50,000 Cr market cap.

5 Check sector rotation

Before entering, check the Rotation tab. If the stock's own industry has already outperformed for 3 months, mean-reversion risk is elevated. If the industry just flipped to outperforming in the last 5 days (Inflection screen), the tailwind is fresh.

6 Log the entry with the conviction snapshot

Use the Position Tracker so future-you can audit which conviction level produced which outcome. This is the learning loop — the tool is only as good as your feedback into it.
